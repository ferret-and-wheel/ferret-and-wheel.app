Mumoir

Speak your memories. Mumoir turns them into a written memoir.

Coming soon to iPhone

  • Just talk

    Press record and tell the story the way you’d tell it at the kitchen table.

  • Written for you

    Each recording becomes a readable note, with the original audio kept alongside it.

  • Private by design

    Transcription happens on your iPhone. Your memories are never sent to us.

  • Synced with iCloud

    Memories are backed up to your own iCloud and stay in step across your devices.

  • Organised in chapters

    Group memories into chapters as the story grows.

  • Share the story

    Export memories as PDF, RTF or Markdown to print or send to family.

Languages

Mumoir works in English and Russian, both for the app itself and for transcription.
